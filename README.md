# Dr. K's Alien Feeding Frenzy 2: Galaxy Gobble

Volume 2 of [Dr. K's Alien Feeding Frenzy](https://ploug76.github.io/alien-crunch/) (Volume 1 is unchanged). A FIVE8NINE Studios game.

**Play:** https://ploug76.github.io/alien-crunch-vol-2/

Emperor Gobblax beamed up Dr. K's Mega Snack Ray, and Zorp has to feed the alien food chain across five neon worlds to win it back.

- 5 worlds (Neon City, Slime Moon, Lava Planet, Crystal Comet, The Mothership), 30 stages: feasts, puzzles and a boss at the end of each world
- 5 bosses: Chompatron, Queen Gloopa, Magmaw, Frostbyte and Emperor Gobblax, each with its own attack (zaps, goo, rumbles, freezes, or all four)
- Painted worlds and story art, the original glowing aliens, Zorp's grabber tongue, particles, screen shake and an original WebAudio score with a track for every world and boss
- Story intro and ending (with Skip), world map with stars and saves, Puzzle Tower, Endless mode
- Touch: swipe and hold to move, tap to grab and spit. Keyboard and game controllers (USB or Bluetooth) work too. No on-screen D-pad.
- Works offline once loaded (PWA) and updates itself on open.

Engine adapted from Kelly Krunch: The Great Feast Quest.
